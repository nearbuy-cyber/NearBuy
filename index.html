Open help centre
What is NearBuy?

NearBuy is a neighbourhood commerce platform that connects customers with local shops and short-distance delivery partners. It brings nearby stores, everyday products and Runner delivery together in one place.

How can I find nearby shops?

Open the customer portal and choose your neighbourhood. NearBuy shows the shops that are serviceable to you, with distance, timing and what they sell.

How do I register my shop?

Use the seller portal to create a shop profile and add your catalogue, timings and delivery radius. In this demo you can explore a fully working Seller Hub instantly — verification is simulated.

How can I become a delivery partner?

Register through the delivery partner portal, complete eligibility and identity verification, then go online. You choose your distance, parcel categories and availability at any time.

Does NearBuy offer home delivery?

Yes. When you choose delivery at checkout, the shop prepares your order and NearBuy Runner matches an eligible nearby partner. You get a delivery OTP to confirm handover.

Can I collect my order directly?

Yes. Store pickup is always free. Choose pickup at checkout, wait for the ready notification, and collect with your order details.

How are delivery charges calculated?

Delivery is priced in short distance bands from the shop to you. In this demo, delivery is free above a small order value and pickup is always free; no real payment is ever taken.

How can I contact support?

Open the help centre from the customer or partner portal. This prototype stores support notes locally only — it does not contact a real support team.
