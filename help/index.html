FAQs

Common questions.

Eight answers that cover most of the platform. For anything else, use the support form inside the portal.

Contact support
What is NearBuy?

NearBuy is a neighbourhood commerce platform connecting customers with local shops and short-distance delivery partners. Nearby stores, everyday products and Runner delivery live in one place.

How do I find shops near me?

Open the customer portal and choose your neighbourhood. NearBuy lists the shops serviceable to your area with distance, timing and categories.

Is my area serviceable?

This demo is set in Hauz Khas, South Delhi. In the real product, serviceability is decided by shop delivery radius and partner coverage. Switching location in the demo shows the same sample catalogue.

How do I register my shop?

Use the seller portal to create a shop profile, add a catalogue, timings and delivery radius. In this prototype, verification is simulated and a demo Seller Hub opens instantly.

How can I become a delivery partner?

Register through the delivery partner portal, complete the demo eligibility check, then go online. You choose your distance, parcel categories and availability.

How are delivery charges calculated?

Delivery is priced in short distance bands from shop to customer. In this demo, delivery is free above a small order value and store pickup is always free. No real payment is ever taken.

What if an order goes wrong?

Use the support form in the customer or partner portal. This prototype stores support notes locally only — it does not contact a real support team or process refunds.

Are these real accounts and payments?

No. Everything here is a working prototype: demo accounts, simulated KYC, OTPs, earnings and dispatch. No real money moves and no documents are collected.

Still stuck?

Write to the team, or open the support form inside the portal. Demo support notes are saved in your browser so you can see the full loop.
