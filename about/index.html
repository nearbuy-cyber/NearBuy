Built for the street you actually live on.

NearBuy is a neighbourhood commerce platform. It brings the shops around the corner online, gives shopkeepers the tools to run and grow, and lets nearby partners earn from short, local deliveries — with one shared record underneath.

Why we exist

Big platforms made local shopping anonymous.

Delivery apps are built to move products from distant warehouses as fast as possible. The shop at the end of your lane — the one that knows your family, keeps your brand of milk and opens early — rarely gets a look in.

NearBuy flips that. We start with a small serviceable radius, list the shops that are genuinely nearby, and let you choose home delivery or a free store pickup. When you order, the money and the relationship stay in the neighbourhood.

For shopkeepers, that means a storefront, a catalogue and an order queue without a website project. For delivery partners, it means short routes with the earning shown before you accept.

What we are building towards

A dense, walkable network: more neighbourhoods, more categories, faster local delivery — and a business model that does not depend on squeezing the shopkeeper.

What we believe

Local first is a design decision, not a slogan.

Every screen is shaped by these three commitments.

Proximity over speed

We optimise for the nearest shop that has what you need, not the fastest truck from the edge of the city.

Honest by default

Distances, delivery charges and partner payouts are shown plainly — before you order, accept or register.

People stay visible

Shops keep their name, hours and character. Partners see the route. Customers see who they are buying from.

One platform, three portals

Separate doors. Shared foundation.

Customers, sellers and delivery partners each get an experience designed for their job — with independent sign-in, verification and dashboards on the same backend.
